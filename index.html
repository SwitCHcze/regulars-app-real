Regulars Student App
Regulars

The same small group, once a week, for something easy.

Students who find it hard to make friends are often the least likely to go looking. So Regulars reaches them through their university: halls, welcome week, Res Life and the wellbeing team. Groups are up to six, the same people every week.

Free for students. Universities and halls pay a fee per term.

What your group can and can’t see
Your group sees

Your first name, your initial, what you said you’d fancy, and whether you’re coming this week.

Your group never sees

Your email, why you joined, your picture, anything you tell the Regulars team privately, or any concern you report.

Your university sees

Only totals, like how many students came this week. Never your name or your answers.

          How it works

          Can’t make it some weeks?

          Any week, no reason needed, no limit. A day’s notice helps the group, but it’s not a rule.

          Need a break?

          Pause in your profile. Your place is kept.

          Missed some without saying?

          It happens. You may get one friendly message. Nobody else is told.

          Keeping your place
          Groups need people who come

          If we haven’t heard from you for a while, we’ll ask if you’d like to keep your place.

          If you don’t reply

          Your place may go to someone waiting, so the group stays big enough. A person decides, never the app, and we tell you first.

          You can always come back

          Any time, even mid-term. Your old group may be full, so you might be offered a different one.

          Each week
          Two options, same time and place

          Your group can choose between two approved activities. No endless voting: if nobody minds, or it’s a tie, the first one goes ahead.

          Small weeks still happen

          If only a few people can come, a hosted session usually still goes ahead. If it has to be cancelled, you’re told and offered an alternative.
